Rotation Settings

Rotation capacity is extra above Normal Max Tabs. Unused rotation capacity can be borrowed by normal streams.

Capacity example
Normal Max Tabs 4 + Rotation Slots 2 means TTM may use up to 6 managed stream tabs. If only one rotation stream needs a slot, the spare rotation slot may temporarily hold another normal stream.
